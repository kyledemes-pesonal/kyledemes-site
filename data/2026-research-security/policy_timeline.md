# Policy timeline: Canadian research security and international collaboration

Verified 2026-11-16. Every URL below was opened; search snippets were not used as sources. "Flag" marks where the brief was wrong, needs care, or couldn't be confirmed.

## Corrections to the brief (read first)

1. **14 Feb 2023 statement: the ministers were Champagne, Duclos and *Mendicino*, not LeBlanc.** LeBlanc signed the 16 Jan 2024 statement, alongside Champagne and Holland (not Boissonnault).
2. **The NSGRP pilot numbers (1,158 / 48 / 32) cover one year, 23 Jul 2021 to 23 Jul 2022**, not 2021–2023. The report itself is titled "2021-2023". 678 of the 1,158 applications were funded.
3. **Budget 2022: $125M over 5 years plus $25M ongoing is the Research Support Fund research-security top-up**, not the whole envelope. The total was $159.6M over 5 years plus $33.4M ongoing, which includes $34.6M plus $8.4M ongoing for research security protection and the Research Security Centre.
4. **Research Security Centre (Public Safety): there is no single creation date.** It was funded in Budget 2022 (Apr 2022), "established and fully operationalized" in June 2023 (PS 2023–24 Departmental Results Report), and "officially launched" in January 2024 (PS evaluation; also the 16 Jan 2024 ministers' statement).
5. **STRAC did not take effect on one date for every agency** (see the table below). The tri-agency trigger is funding opportunities *launched* on or after 1 May 2024, not applications *submitted* after that date. CFI phased it in by fund.
6. **There was no substantive STRAC update on 6 Oct 2026.** "Date modified: 2026-10-06" on the policy, STRA list and NSGRP pages is cosmetic. Wayback diffs (Jul/Aug vs Oct 2026 snapshots) show only the removal of a "2026 Research Security Survey is now open" banner.
   - The STRA list text has not changed since May 2024: 11 categories.
   - The NRO list page was last modified 2024-04-18: 103 entries, 85 China, 12 Iran, 6 Russia (counted from the live page).
   - The last substantive policy change was **2025-05-22**. It added a definition of "aims to advance" a STRA, replaced the validation section with randomized post-award validation plus parallel validation for NSGRP-referred files, and added annual ISED reporting.
7. **Alberta: the pause was May 2021, not 2022/2023.** It was partly eased in May 2022, when low-risk undergraduate exchange and corporate training were allowed; graduate and doctoral research ties stayed paused.
8. **Ontario: security assessments began in 2019 for ORF-Research Excellence** (confirmed). A risk-mitigation route followed in 2023, and formal guidelines with attestations in June 2024.
9. **Quebec: no 2023 measure was found.** The FRQ runs a STRAC/NSGRP-style *pilot in 2026* (one Nature and technologies partnership program).
10. **Globe and Mail story: 30 Jan 2023 is confirmed.** It was followed by "Ottawa vows to curb..." on 2 Feb 2023. The earlier 29 Oct 2018 Globe piece (ASPI/Joske data) is a separate, earlier story.
11. **CERN:** the June 2022 Council (16 Jun, statement 17 Jun) stated an *intention* to end the agreements. The *decision* came 15 Dec 2023. End dates: Belarus 27 Jun 2024, Russia 30 Nov 2024.
12. **China "breaking SCI worship":** the MOE/MOST document is dated 18 Feb 2020 and was posted 23 Feb 2020.
13. **Couldn't confirm from an official page:**
    - US China Initiative launch month (Nov 2018). justice.gov blocks automated fetches, so the end date (23 Feb 2022) comes from a law-firm write-up.
    - UK Trusted Research launch (~2019). The NPSA/CPNI site returns 403.
    - Germany BMBF research-security position paper: only "2024" confirmed from the ministry page.
    - EU Council press release: the Commission's news page was used instead.
