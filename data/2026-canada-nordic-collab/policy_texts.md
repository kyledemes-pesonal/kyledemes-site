# The 28 government texts behind "Why now?"

The post counts how many of 28 government texts, from 2017 to 2026, name each priority for Canada–Nordic cooperation. The texts are joint statements, agreements, readouts and policies of the Government of Canada with Nordic governments and agencies or the EU (one, the Arctic allies' statement of May 2026, also includes the US), tallied from each text in November 2026. A text counts for a priority if it names it at least once. The Inuit Circumpolar Council's 2026 Iqaluit Declaration is not counted, since it is not a government text.
