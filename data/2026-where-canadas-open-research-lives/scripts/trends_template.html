Free to read means a free copy exists anywhere today (gold, diamond, hybrid, green or bronze), for journal articles and reviews with at least one author at the university or country. A paper counts once for each. 2026 is a partial year (papers published so far, dashed and shaded), and recent years run low because repository copies often arrive after publication.
