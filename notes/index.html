Field notes

Same habit, new habitat. As a kelp ecologist, every study I ran started as scribbles in a waterproof notebook. These days I'm observing the scholarly ecosystem: funders, publishers, universities, and a few species nobody has described yet. I rarely have time to turn these notes into proper studies, so I'm keeping them tidy for the day I do, or for anyone who wants to beat me to it.
